Most of my week starts on the shop floor, not at a whiteboard. I commission, program, and deploy industrial robotic arms, while integrating the sensors, controllers, connectivity, and software around them, so a design on paper becomes a machine that repeats the same motion, correctly, thousands of times without anyone babysitting it.

Giving RTBS a face — and a memory

I led the architecture behind RTBS, an AI face‑recognition ticketing platform, and built it to scale from a single walk‑in to a large group moving through access control at once. The harder problem was making it feel effortless: I layered in gamified engagement, tighter workflows, and progressive unlocks so the system rewarded people for showing up instead of just checking them in.

Ideas that survive past the prototype

Mentoring the next build
