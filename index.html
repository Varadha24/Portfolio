Robobooth

Automated Robotic Videography System
01

A robot arm that films you like a movie scene. A Doosan 6-axis cobot moves through the shot while my FastAPI backend runs the show: arm motion through DART, camera recording through ADB, and an Arduino relay keeping both in sync. FFmpeg then adds slow motion, branding, and music, so the clip is ready fast. It runs real production sessions, not just on my desk.

Smart Ticketing Platform

Visitor Management & Smart Access for RoboPark
02

A park visit shouldn't start with a queue. At Inker Robotics, I designed the ticketing journey for RoboPark end to end, from the first online booking to the post-visit follow-up. It covers solo and group bookings, face recognition at entry, and companion identification so groups stay connected. Behind it sit the flowcharts, API requirements, notification logic, and data schemas, plus a gamification layer of points, leaderboards, and rally competitions.

Campus Monitoring & Digital Twin

What if a campus could tell you how it's doing? ESP32 sensing nodes track attendance, occupancy, environment, energy, parking, and emergencies, then stream live to a web app over WebSocket. A 3D digital twin turns that data into something you can actually see. Every piece is its own subsystem, so the campus can keep growing without a rewrite.

Vision-Based Bionic Hand

Real-Time Gesture Replication
04

Wave at a camera, and a hand waves back. I designed and 3D-printed a 5-joint robotic hand, then taught it to copy mine. MediaPipe tracks the hand landmarks, Python maps gestures to servo angles with inverse kinematics, and UART carries the commands to an Arduino that drives the servos.
